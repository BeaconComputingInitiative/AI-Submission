### Contents

- **6,000 distinct questions/scenarios**, across 72 problem families.
- 1,000 examples each: Scale, Arpeggio, Chorded Truth, Octet Tokenizer, Conductor, Tonal Variants.
- **4,800 training / 600 validation / 600 test** records.
- Multi-factor calculations and constrained judgments rather than single-variable substitutions.
- Explicit provenance, arithmetic, financial-label, and approval/rejection checks.
- Generator, verifier, instruction-export utility, and SHA-256 manifests in the canonical ZIP.

It pushes **if and only if** all validation and repository gates pass.

### Research limits

This is **synthetic instruction-training data**, not validated real financial transactions, real market time series, or evidence of financial forecasting ability. M4.1 PyTorch sequence models do not directly consume these JSONL messages; a distinct numerical feature/target mapping and held-out evaluation are required before further model promotion. The train, validation, and test cohorts are disjoint, but all are generated by one family of procedures; that does not guarantee out-of-distribution generalization.

**A440 model deployment is not approved.**
