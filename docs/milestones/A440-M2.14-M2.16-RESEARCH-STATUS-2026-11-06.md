## Open engineering gates

1. Implement a typed, multi-resolution semantic-to-neural compiler for **nonadditive rates**, **logarithmic event magnitudes**, **monthly mixed statistics**, and **monthly percentages**. Do not force these into the original daily-additive feature contract.
2. Reserve genuinely new chronological and cross-domain evaluation windows; avoid optimizing repeatedly against the previously viewed calibration set.
3. Diagnose semantic feature utility (including ablation in meaningful in-distribution conditions), training instability, gradient conflict and cross-domain negative transfer.
4. Implement isolated concurrent workers with a durable, verified evidence snapshot boundary, backpressure, crash recovery and research-only candidate training.
5. Keep all prior checkpoints, reports and provenance immutable. Promotion permitted **if and only if** independent quality, integrity, authorization and regression gates pass.
