## Weight-level audit (user-reported actual Kali run)
- **12/12 weight tests passed**, zero failures/errors; read-only to the installed model.
- Source fingerprint `b79c6c73ff85a5ed3c71cc094930827cb260fb82f7b59e4a85730a2d8124976e` matched U2.1.
- Neural validation MAE `0.01737696` vs persistence MAE `0.01508226` on the inspected synthetic restaurant validation period.
- Training tenant A changed tenant B's prediction by `+0.13919793` normalized output units. Shared trunk and role head updates can influence other tenants despite distinct metric-specific residual heads. **This is an isolation/design issue, not proof of unauthorized data access.**
- Feature hash diagnostic: 201 feature labels, 99 occupied buckets out of 124, 60 buckets with collisions; such collisions can merge distinct signals.
- Numerical gradient checks, loss convergence, checkpoint round trip, chronological feature isolation and rejection retaining approved weight bytes passed.
- These tests do **not** show positive cross-industry transfer, production data isolation, causal inference, or an unseen-business performance advantage.

## Research conclusion
The model is functionally trainable and can accept distinct synthetic industry schemas. However, shared-tenant weight interference, feature hashing collisions, and forecasting performance that often trails persistence make automated company CSV or JSONL self-training inappropriate for production. U2.2 **has not been implemented**.

**Next requirements:** freeze shared weights during tenant adaptation, add explicitly versioned tenant adapters, address feature identity collisions, require CSV/JSONL contract and unit validation, use preregistered chronological holdouts, and promote a candidate **if and only if** provenance, privacy, non-regression, comparative accuracy and recovery gates pass.

**User's original product objective:** Infrastructure onboarding after enterprise purchase, daily 00:00 local collection of previous-day evidence, with a reconciled daily intelligence report. This is architectural direction, not implemented infrastructure in the U2.1 research model.
