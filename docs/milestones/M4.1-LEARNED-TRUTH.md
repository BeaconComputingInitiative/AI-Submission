## Research objective

Teach A440 four measurable operational learning responsibilities using eight
past numerical observations and a separately withheld ninth outcome.

- **L09:** Recognize direction or periodic patterns.
- **L10:** Compare whether distinct contextual sequences share a known generating rule.
- **L11:** Predict the next outcome and compare with persistence and extrapolation.
- **L12:** Classify known generating-rule families; no claim of general program synthesis.

One shared numerical encoder feeds four specialized heads. Synthetic environments
used for train/validation/test are disjoint, but rule families overlap. This
is evidence of **within-generator generalization only**.
