## Purpose

Evaluate the existing M4.1 four-head numerical model through M4.2's Chorded
Truth interface, without modifying its weights or its underlying proof claims.

**Three evaluation groups:**

1. **Evidence adversarial checks:** Accept a correct arithmetic declaration,
   contradict an incorrect declaration, reject an altered source record,
   deliberately inject a wrong neural direction, and keep unsupported causal
   conclusions unresolved. A matching self-hash is integrity evidence only,
   not external authenticity or validation of the originating source.
2. **Cross-environment comparison:** Require matching measurement units,
   metric definitions, time periods and accounting basis; classify sector or
   geographical differences as confounders instead of assuming comparability.
   The trained L10 head is scored separately against known synthetic pair labels.
3. **Forecast accountability:** Calibrate forecast intervals on independent
   synthetic domain IDs 8–9, and test on unseen IDs 10–11. Compare the L11
   forecast against persistence and linear extrapolation. The future ninth
   observation remains unavailable to the model during inference.

The report includes observed error metrics, benchmark results, evidence checks,
context-comparison statuses, and a preliminary 90% split-conformal interval.
The interval's empirical coverage does not guarantee future coverage under
nonexchangeable domain shifts.

## Admission and promotion rules

A calculated claim is verified if and only if its source-linked value, metric,
units, schema, and mathematical derivation satisfy all applicable checks.

A context comparison is qualified if and only if compatible measurement units,
metric definition, reporting period, accounting basis and required population
qualifiers are available. Differences in sector or geography require explicit
conditional status; they must not silently become independent proof of trends.

A candidate model is deployable if and only if all *production* integrity,
privacy, authorization, robustness, calibration, regression and governance
checks pass. **M4.3 never sets `deployment_approved` to true.**

## Limitations

- Four mathematical generating-rule families are predefined, not discovered.
- Synthetic data neither establishes real market intelligence nor causal reasoning.
- Self-hashing does not provide a cryptographic signature from a trusted issuer.
- The injected wrong-model test measures an explicit contradiction detector,
  not real-model sensitivity under realistic distribution shift.
- L12 rule-family classification is not arbitrary program synthesis.
- The current M4.2 Chord checks limited numerical invariants and no external data.
- Calibration and evaluation domains are disjoint, but interval coverage may
  be unreliable if their distributions differ.
- No tenant data flows, real-time ingestion, online learning, or deployment.

## Next
